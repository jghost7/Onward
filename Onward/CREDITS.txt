Quest data
  The route guides in Guides/Draft/ are generated from the quest, NPC, object and item
  database of Questie (https://github.com/Questie/Questie), licensed under GPL-3.0.
  Routes are computed by tools/draft_guide.py in Onward's source repository, not authored
  by hand, and not taken from any other guide addon.

Optional integrations
  Questie and TomTom are used when installed; neither is bundled.

Icons
  UI icons in media/ are rendered from Lucide (https://lucide.dev), licensed under the ISC License.

Source
  The addon ships as plain Lua: every file in this folder is its own source code.
